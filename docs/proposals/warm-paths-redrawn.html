A proposal for the studio · Warm intros · 30 Sep 2026

Warm paths, redrawn

The engine under Warm intros is better than the screen over it. A search already verifies each tie against a page it read, cites it, ranks the paths and writes the two drafts CTD sells as its headline feature. The screen shows one empty field. It never says what network it is searching, answers nothing until a three-minute run is over, files the answer in a log under the form, and once the drafts exist nothing happens next.

CTD's shape is the reverse: the network is a visible thing with a size, a search answers instantly from it, a path is an object with a stage, and the research is a second press. This keeps the engine and every privacy rule and rebuilds the screen in that order. The look is the Origami shape and nothing else; CTD decides what the screen does, never how it looks.

Drawn in the studio's own tokens. Nothing below is built. Names on the screens are examples.

Why the screen reads as random

Read against the 30 Sep screenshot of /intro and the code on main at 0d3847a. The operator: "right now it's zero clear how to use it and what to do."

The field floats in nothing.

The screen searches a network it never shows. GET /api/intro already answers network.imported, and renderIntro reads only network.ready from it. A member with 312 connections and one with a pasted list of two see the same empty field.

The instant half is hidden.

matchNetwork answers who do you know there from the imports alone: no model, milliseconds, free. It is drawn as Already in your network on a finished pitch's panel and nowhere else. On /intro, typing changes nothing on the page, and the only answer costs a run and takes up to three minutes.

The field is a plain text box.

Every other company box in the product is the suggest combo. This one is <input type="text">, so a typed name reaches the engine with website: null.

The answer is a log.

Results are What you have asked, the twelve newest jobs, each a list and a folded draft. Press, see Searching your network… under the form, and three minutes later a list lands there. No count, no headline, no state.

Nothing happens after the draft.

Two drafts, two Copy buttons. No asked, no they said yes, no meeting, no hand-off to Outcomes. CTD's product is what happens after the path is found. Ours ends at the clipboard.

The network has no shape.

Your network is an importer. It still wears the Teach eyebrow two days after #877 moved it under Warm intros, an import is a row with a count, and the five tiers strengthOf computes (strong, warm, light, unknown, one-way) are shown nowhere.

The second direction sits under a seller's field.

Or find someone worth meeting for a contact you know is an investor's or an agency's job, and the only sentence on the screen besides the title. It explains the wrong thing first.

The machine surface needs a pitch.

find_warm_paths takes a pitchId only, so a Claude asked who do we know at Harborline has to buy an eleven-minute pitch first. CTD's MCP answers a company, a person or a connector directly.

  • What already works and stays: every path starts with somebody in the network; every tie is verified and cited; strong or possible; at most five; a budget sized on the connectors and a three-minute clock (#609); an honest empty answer; the two drafts under 100 words (#356); the tally tiers (#355); the agency lending (#353) and the roll-up (#452); no addresses stored, no OAuth, the studio contacts nobody, nothing on a share.

What CTD does, and what transfers

Read 30 Sep 2026 off ctd.ai/venture-capital, ctd.ai/integrations-mcp and the public guide. Their own five steps: onboard the network (email by OAuth, LinkedIn as the member's own CSV), build the graph (four tiers, one-way traffic scores zero), search any target instantly and read a count line first ("142 warm paths · 31 strong"), activate (the ghost email the connector sends; a path with a pipeline stage; tracked conversion), expand (external members, lists, job changes, an MCP with fifteen verbs). The mechanism teardown is docs/research/ctd-teardown.md (#354) and is not repeated.
